## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one gestural trace, not a sketch of the whole object and not a logo template. A stroke, a color patch or a fragment of contour must carry the identity. Breaks, fades and missing parts are allowed. Use charcoal, ink or graphite with speed and direction, on warm white, plus one scarce emotional color from the photograph. The mark stays small and isolated. Type is a tiny archive number. Reject a finished illustration, a commercial logo, cartoon, decoration and realism.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
